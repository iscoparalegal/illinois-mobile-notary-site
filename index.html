NNA Certified Means Background Checked

Your notary has passed a background check. Peace of mind before we ever walk through your door.

You are trusting a notary with your ID, your signature and often your home or a hospital room. Our certification from the National Notary Association means an independent organization has run a background check on us and tested our knowledge.

What certification means

  • Background checked

    Passed a background check that meets the standards used by title companies, lenders and signing services.

  • Trained and tested

    Completed National Notary Association training and passed its certification exam on proper signing procedures.

  • Renewed every year

    The background check is repeated every year to keep the certification active.

  • Bonded and insured

    Illinois commissioned, with a $30,000 notary bond and $100,000 errors and omissions insurance.

Common questions

How fast can you get here?

Often the same day, and frequently within a few hours. Call or text with your location for the earliest time.

What ID do I need?

A current government photo ID with your signature, such as a driver's license, state ID or passport.

Can you tell me what to sign?

No. Notaries cannot give legal advice. We verify identity and witness signatures.
