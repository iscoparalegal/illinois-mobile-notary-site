Signing agent services

We handle loan signings across Chicagoland for title companies, escrow officers, lenders and signing services. We arrive prepared, guide borrowers through where to sign, date and initial, check every page before leaving, and return documents on time.

Packages we sign

  • Purchase and buyer packages
  • Seller packages and deeds
  • Refinances
  • HELOCs and home equity loans
  • Reverse mortgages
  • Loan modifications
  • Hybrid closings, where eligible

For title companies and signing services

  • Available 24/7, including evenings and weekends when borrowers are home
  • Printing of letter and legal packages
  • Scanbacks when requested
  • Same day delivery or overnight shipping with tracking
  • Status updates by text or email at confirmation, arrival and completion
  • NNA Certified and background screened
  • $100,000 E&O insurance and a $30,000 bond

How an assignment works

  1. Assignment

    Send the borrower details, location, time and return instructions.

  2. Confirm and prepare

    We confirm with the borrower, print the package and review it before the signing.

  3. Sign and return

    We complete the signing, check every page, scan back if requested and ship or drop off.

For borrowers

Your lender, title company or escrow officer usually schedules the signing agent. If you would like us at your closing, ask them to contact us directly.

Our role. A Notary Signing Agent identifies signers, presents documents for signature and notarizes them. We cannot explain loan terms, rates or fees, or give legal or financial advice. Questions about your loan go to your lender, and legal questions go to your attorney.
